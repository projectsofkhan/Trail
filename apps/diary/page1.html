The Revelation

I chased by Shadow, all the day

Until the light was taken away.

Then in the dark I came to see,

My shadow was just part of me.


The truth reveals itself in absence,

What follows you in every instance,

Grows tall when light begins to fade,

Yet in the darkness, doesn't shade.


It mimics every move you make,

But never gives or takes,

In brightest light, it's close at hand,

In total dark, it takes a stand.
